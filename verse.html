Anxious

Philippians 4:6

WEB
“Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God.”

What this means

Paul reminds us that we don’t have to carry our worries alone. We can bring everything to God in prayer, with a heart of trust and gratitude.

Reflect for a moment

  • What is worrying you most right now?
  • Can you surrender it to God in prayer today?
  • What is one thing you are thankful for even in this situation?

Who wrote this

The apostle Paul wrote to the church in Philippi while he was in prison. Even then, his letter is full of joy and encouragement.

Where it fits

This verse sits in Paul’s closing words, where he calls believers to rejoice, be gentle and pray. The promise that follows in verse 7 is God’s peace guarding the heart and mind.
